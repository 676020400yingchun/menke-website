ABOUT · WHO IS MENKE

A Name, an Ancient Bond of Trust

BRAND STORY · THE NAME "MENKE" The Warring States kept retainers —
three thousand MENKE,
each with their own craft

The name "MENKE" comes from the Warring States tradition of keeping retainers. Three thousand retainers, each with their own craft, counseled their enlightened lords and repaid recognition with talent. Today's MENKE carries on precisely this ancient bond of trust.

We are not aloof "vendors" but a seated guest who steps into your arena — entering your door, learning your trade, solving your problems, and walking with you until the work is done.

Retainers of old repaid recognition with talent; the MENKE of today honors promises with expertise.
